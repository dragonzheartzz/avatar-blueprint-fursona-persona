# Avatar Blueprint: Roadmap

**Last updated:** 19 November 2026  
**Current stage:** Repository foundation and historical documentation consolidation  
**Overall status:** Concept-stage project. No implementation or commercial launch authorised by this roadmap.

## Purpose

This roadmap develops Avatar Blueprint as a standalone, customer-facing fursona/persona-to-avatar planning service. It keeps the blueprint service distinct from the related Auto-Avatars production project.

Roadmap stages are proposed sequencing, not automatic authority to execute. Each gate requires an explicit decision where the work would move beyond currently authorised documentation and research.

## Stage 1: Service definition and requirements

**Status: PROPOSED / CEO DECISION REQUIRED**

Define the service sufficiently to determine what a customer supplies, what we promise, what we deliver and what remains outside scope.

Candidate work:
- Select the first customer segment and narrow initial use case.
- Define intake routes for a new fursona/persona concept, an existing specification, and an existing avatar requiring additions or redesign.
- Define the blueprint schema and customer-facing report format.
- Define supported avatar ecosystem(s), source categories and permitted research methods.
- Specify compatibility evidence levels, confidence language and verification boundaries.
- Define asset provenance, licence and attribution checks.
- Define price/availability freshness and how uncertainty is presented.
- Define optional production hand-off without making it a dependency.
- Identify customer-data, privacy, consent and retention requirements.
- Define delivery-time, quality, correction and support measures.

**Gate:** CEO decision on whether to authorise bounded requirements/specification work. The historical 28 October recommendation to advance to Athena planning is a recommendation, not proof that approval was given.

## Stage 2: Source and competitor validation

**Status: PROPOSED / NOT AUTHORISED BY THIS ROADMAP**

- Compare selected discovery tools and creator services using one common persona brief.
- Verify current features, catalogue coverage, prices, terms and access methods from primary sources.
- Identify which source gaps matter to the chosen customer segment.
- Test whether persona-led synthesis adds value beyond free search/filter tools.
- Preserve negative findings and limitations.

**Gate:** evidence supports a distinctive, compliant and useful research workflow worth testing further.

## Stage 3: Manual blueprint prototype and delivery-economics assessment

**Status: FUTURE / REQUIRES SEPARATE APPROVAL**

- Design a repeatable internal checklist and report template.
- If specifically authorised, prepare a bounded manual example using permitted public sources.
- Measure research time, completeness, compatibility confidence, stale-data risk and correction burden.
- Assess whether the customer outcome can be delivered consistently at a plausible price.
- Establish explicit pass/revise/stop thresholds before any real customer test.

**Gate:** demonstrated delivery quality and plausible unit economics, not enthusiasm alone.

## Stage 4: Customer validation

**Status: FUTURE / REQUIRES SEPARATE APPROVAL**

- Design an appropriately consented, privacy-conscious test.
- Test a clearly described offer with a defined customer segment.
- Measure actual task value, credible willingness to pay, objections, support needs and preference for independent versus creator-led delivery.
- Avoid treating compliments, clicks or hypothetical interest as purchase evidence.

**Gate:** evidence of a real customer problem and a viable bounded offer.

## Stage 5: Repeatable service operations

**Status: FUTURE / DEPENDENT ON VALIDATION**

- Standardise intake, research, quality review, report generation and delivery.
- Establish source monitoring and re-check rules for prices, availability, licences and compatibility.
- Define customer correction, refund and support handling.
- Decide what should remain human-reviewed and what can safely be assisted by software.
- Assess operating cost and sustainable capacity.

**Gate:** repeatable service quality, clear rights boundaries and workable economics.

## Stage 6: Optional production hand-off

**Status: FUTURE / SEPARATE PROJECT DEPENDENCY**

- Inspect Auto-Avatars' verified capabilities and interface at the time of consideration.
- Define a structured hand-off manifest only if the production project can genuinely consume it.
- Require customer selection, asset rights and explicit production approval.
- Identify unsupported work and manual steps rather than implying end-to-end automation.
- Keep the blueprint independently useful if production integration is unavailable.

**Gate:** verified technical interface, agreed scope, customer permission and separate project approval.

## Stage 7: Commercial decision and controlled launch

**Status: FUTURE / REQUIRES CEO APPROVAL**

- Confirm target segment, service scope, prices, delivery capacity and customer terms.
- Confirm rights, privacy, support and refund arrangements.
- Decide whether the evidence supports launch, another validation cycle, a narrower offer, or stopping.
- Record the CEO decision and launch boundaries.

**Gate:** explicit CEO approval. No roadmap entry alone authorises public launch or commercial commitment.

## Cross-cutting principles

1. **Standalone value:** the blueprint must remain useful without buying production from us.
2. **Customer choice:** recommendations and alternatives must be inspectable; the customer owns final choices.
3. **Evidence-led compatibility:** never turn an inference into a guarantee.
4. **Rights and provenance:** preserve creator links, terms, permissions and source dates.
5. **Freshness:** prices, listings, versions and licences can change.
6. **Fail-first discipline:** free substitutes, no standalone buyer, labour burden and poor source coverage are valid reasons to revise or stop.
7. **Project separation:** Auto-Avatars is a potential downstream service, not a prerequisite for the initial blueprint.
8. **Governance:** capability is not authority; each stage remains subject to Tilly's decisions and applicable governance.
