# Avatar Blueprint: Roadmap

**Last updated:** 19 November 2026  
**Current stage:** Stage 1 concept definition (working vision recorded; detailed requirements remain open)  
**Overall status:** Concept-stage project. No implementation or commercial launch authorised by this roadmap.

## Purpose

This roadmap develops Avatar Blueprint as a personalised, human-and-AI collaborative service that helps customers turn a fursona/persona idea into a character they can love and a practical plan for bringing it into existence. It keeps the blueprint service distinct from the related Auto-Avatars production project.

Roadmap stages are proposed sequencing, not automatic authority to execute. Each gate requires an explicit decision where the work would move beyond currently authorised documentation and research.

## Stage 1: Service definition and requirements

**Status: IN DISCUSSION / WORKING VISION DOCUMENTED 19 NOVEMBER 2026; DETAILED REQUIREMENTS OPEN**

Tilly and Metis have agreed a current concept-level service vision, recorded in the README. It is a working direction to guide further discussion and decisions, not a completed operational specification or authority for implementation.

Agreed direction:
- The service supports customers at different starting points: an early idea, an existing character concept/sheet, an existing avatar, or a precise brief for discovery or creation.
- Customers can choose case by case how involved they want us to be: delegate a clear brief, collaborate with guidance, or request a focused research/asset task.
- The service is an honest one-to-one human-and-AI collaboration. We must explain what is automated, where human judgement is involved and what has or has not been verified.
- Fursona and persona templates should be available as useful starting resources, included with the initial paid service rather than advertised as a free standalone offer. They should help shape a brief, not become a compulsory barrier.
- Repeatable asset research, lists, links, prices and source records should be increasingly automated through structured templates and processes, with appropriate human review.
- Service levels and fees should reflect the amount of creative involvement, research depth, technical assessment, custom work and support. Exact tiers and prices are not yet defined.
- The service can progress from idea to character sheet, visual blueprint and costing, then optionally to individually created assets or a separate Auto-Avatars production service. Customers may enter at any point and stop with a useful standalone deliverable.
- The emotional objective is to take the customer's character seriously, respect their creative ownership and help them achieve something personal they can love and grow attached to.
- The blueprint must remain useful independently and customers remain free to use any creator.
- Pheme is responsible for consumer research and market intelligence. Iris is responsible for advertising and marketing. No specialist has been commissioned by this discussion.

Remaining candidate requirements work:
- Define customer intake and briefing routes, including when a template is useful versus conversation or delegation.
- Define service levels, deliverables, scope boundaries, revision/support limits and fee logic.
- Define character-sheet and blueprint schemas and customer-facing formats.
- Define supported avatar ecosystem(s), source categories and permitted research methods.
- Specify compatibility evidence levels, confidence language and verification boundaries.
- Define asset provenance, licence and attribution checks.
- Define price/availability freshness and how uncertainty is presented.
- Define optional custom-asset and Auto-Avatars production hand-offs, including approvals and separate quotes.
- Identify customer-data, privacy, consent and retention requirements.
- Define human review, automation boundaries, delivery-time, quality and correction measures.
- Capture customer language, motivations, pain points, objections and willingness-to-pay evidence through the appropriate research remit.

**Next gate:** Tilly decides whether and how to progress from this shared concept into a bounded requirements/specification exercise. No Athena hand-off or specialist commission has been made. The historical 28 October recommendation to advance to Athena planning remains a recommendation, not approval.

## Cross-cutting principles

1. **Customer ownership:** the character and creative direction belong to the customer.
2. **Flexible involvement:** support delegated briefs, guided collaboration and focused tasks according to the customer's preference.
3. **Honest human/AI collaboration:** explain automation, human judgement, limits and verification clearly.
4. **Emotional care:** take the customer's idea seriously and help them achieve a character they value.
5. **Standalone value:** the blueprint remains useful without buying custom production from us.
6. **Customer choice:** recommendations and alternatives must be inspectable; customers can use any creator.
7. **Evidence-led compatibility:** never turn an inference into a guarantee.
8. **Rights and provenance:** preserve creator links, terms, permissions and source dates.
9. **Automation with review:** automate repeatable work where reliable; retain appropriate human oversight.
10. **Freshness:** prices, listings, versions and licences can change.
11. **Fail-first discipline:** free substitutes, no standalone buyer, labour burden and poor source coverage are valid reasons to revise or stop.
12. **Project separation:** Auto-Avatars is a potential downstream service, not a prerequisite for the initial blueprint.
13. **Research/marketing remit:** Pheme owns consumer research and market intelligence; Iris owns advertising and marketing.
14. **Governance:** capability is not authority; each stage remains subject to Tilly's decisions and applicable governance.
