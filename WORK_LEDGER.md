# Avatar Blueprint: Work Ledger

This ledger records material project history, decisions and the provenance of documents brought into this repository. It distinguishes historical work, current concept-level agreement, recommendations and formal authorisation.

## Status key

- **RECORDED:** historical event/document verified in its source.
- **AGREED CONCEPT:** CEO and Metis have aligned on a working direction; this is not automatically implementation authority.
- **RECOMMENDED:** a recommendation was made; it is not approval.
- **PROPOSED:** candidate next work, not authorised by inclusion here.
- **IN PROGRESS:** current bounded work is underway.
- **VERIFIED:** outcome checked against the stated evidence.
- **BLOCKED / UNVERIFIED:** evidence or authority is insufficient.

### AB-2026-11-19-003: Shared service vision documented

**Date:** 19 November 2026  
**Status:** AGREED CONCEPT / DOCUMENTATION VERIFIED  
**Decision context:** Tilly and Metis discussed the service vision directly. Tilly clarified that no work had been sent to Athena and that the project remained in CEO/Metis ideation. Tilly then authorised documenting the shared direction and updating existing repository documentation rather than creating a duplicate brief.

**Agreed working direction:**
- Avatar Blueprint is a personalised, one-to-one human-and-AI collaborative service that helps customers turn a fursona/persona idea into a character they can love and a practical plan for realising it.
- Customers may arrive with an early idea, existing character sheet, existing avatar or a precise task brief.
- The service flexes around the customer's preferred involvement: delegated brief, guided collaboration, focused discovery or creative development.
- Fursona and persona templates should be available as useful starting resources, included with the initial paid service rather than advertised as a free standalone offer. They help customers reflect and shape a brief but are not compulsory.
- Repeatable asset research, lists, source links, prices and records should be increasingly automated through structured templates and processes, with appropriate human review.
- Service fees should reflect the work involved, including creative participation, research depth, technical assessment, custom work and support. Exact tiers, deliverables and prices remain open.
- The customer journey may move from idea to character sheet, visual blueprint and costing, then optionally to custom asset creation or the separate Auto-Avatars production service. Customers may enter at any stage and stop with a useful standalone deliverable.
- The emotional objective is to take the customer's character seriously, respect their creative ownership and help them achieve something personal they can love and grow attached to.
- The service must be transparent about its human-and-AI collaboration, automation, human judgement, limitations and verification.
- The blueprint remains independently useful; customers are free to use any creator.
- Pheme owns consumer research and market intelligence. Iris owns advertising and marketing. Neither specialist has been commissioned by this discussion.

**Documentation updated:** README.md (current service brief) and ROADMAP.md (Stage 1 working vision and remaining requirements). Historical source documents remain intact.

**Authority boundary:** This records a shared concept-level direction and authorises its documentation only. It does not constitute a completed requirements specification, Athena hand-off, specialist commission, implementation approval, customer test, spending approval, asset purchase, marketplace integration or launch approval.
