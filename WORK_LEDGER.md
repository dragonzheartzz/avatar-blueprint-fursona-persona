## Status key

- **RECORDED:** historical event/document verified in its source.
- **RECOMMENDED:** a recommendation was made; it is not approval.
- **PROPOSED:** candidate next work, not authorised by inclusion here.
- **IN PROGRESS:** current bounded work is underway.
- **VERIFIED:** outcome checked against the stated evidence.
- **BLOCKED / UNVERIFIED:** evidence or authority is insufficient.
